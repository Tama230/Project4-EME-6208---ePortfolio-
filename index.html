Master of Curriculum and Instruction - University of Florida - EME 6208

Hello!! I'm Tama, a geography teacher who genuinely loves exploring how technology can actually make learning feel alive for students. Lately, I've been diving deep into designing better learning management systems and figuring out how digital media can truly transform everyday classrooms into immersive spaces. This portfolio is basically my personal creative workshop for EME 6208 at the University of Florida; a space where I get to test out ideas, build interactive tools, and share projects that bridge spatial concepts with modern tech. Take your time looking around, and I hope you enjoy checking out my journey as much as I enjoyed putting it all together!
